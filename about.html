Our Story

Hotel Rukhmini Palace was founded with a single vision — to bring world-class hospitality to the heart of Kolhapur. Nestled along the scenic Nippani-Radhanagri road, our property has been a home-away-from-home for travellers, families, and business guests for over a decade.

Every room, every dish, and every interaction is crafted with care — because we believe that true luxury is not just about opulence, it is about feeling genuinely welcomed. Our staff is trained to anticipate your needs before you voice them.

From our grand event hall to our tranquil AC suites, every corner of Rukhmini Palace has been designed to offer comfort, elegance, and an experience that you will return to again and again.
